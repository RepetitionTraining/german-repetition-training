Tips

  • Relative clauses provide additional information about a noun

  • Nominative relative pronouns: der, die, das, die (plural)

  • Used when the relative pronoun is the subject of the relative clause

  • The verb in the relative clause goes to the end

  • Relative pronouns have the same forms as definite articles in nominative

  • The relative clause is separated by commas

Examples#

Das ist der Mann, der Deutsch spricht.
That’s the man who speaks German.

Kennst du die Frau, die dort steht?
Do you know the woman who stands there?

Das Buch, das auf dem Tisch gelegen hat, gehörte mir.
The book that lay on the table belonged to me.

Die Kinder, die im Garten gespielt haben, waren laut.
The children who played in the garden were loud.

Tom ist der Student, der immer pünktlich ist.
Tom is the student who is always punctual.

Cologne is the city that lies on the Rhine.

The car that stands in front of the house is new.

The people who lived here were very nice.

The restaurant that was around the corner was very good.

What was the name of the film that ran yesterday?

The colleague who sat next to me came from Berlin.

The house that has a garden is expensive.

The teacher who taught mathematics was strict.

The music that came from the radio was beautiful.

When does the bus that goes to Berlin come?

The café that opened at 7 o’clock made good coffee.

The students who had passed the exam were happy.

The train that went to Munich was delayed.

Süß ist die Katze, die auf dem Sofa schläft.
The cat that sleeps on the sofa is cute.

The picture that hung on the wall was old.

Der Arzt, der im Krankenhaus gearbeitet hat, war erfahren.
The doctor who worked in the hospital was experienced.

Die Nachbarn, die laut waren, haben mich gestört.
The neighbors who were loud disturbed me.

Das Fenster, das offen gestanden hat, musste geschlossen werden.
The window that was open had to be closed.

Neu ist der Computer, der auf dem Schreibtisch steht.
The computer that stands on the desk is new.

Die Blumen, die im Garten gewachsen sind, waren schön.
The flowers that grew in the garden were beautiful.

Das Problem, das existiert, ist ernst.
The problem that exists is serious.

Der Schlüssel, der auf dem Tisch liegt, ist meiner.
The key that lies on the table is mine.

Die Sprache, die schwer ist, braucht viel Übung.
The language that is difficult needs a lot of practice.

Das Fenster, das kaputt ist, wird repariert.
The window that is broken is being repaired.

Der Laden, der billig ist, ist immer voll.
The store that is cheap is always full.

Die Idee, die gut ist, wird umgesetzt.
The idea that is good is being implemented.

Das Handy, das klingelt, gehört dir.
The phone that is ringing belongs to you.

Der Park, der groß ist, liegt in der Stadtmitte.
The park that is big is in the city center.

Die Aufgabe, die schwierig ist, dauert lange.
The task that is difficult takes a long time.

Das Mädchen, das Klavier spielt, ist talentiert.
The girl who plays piano is talented.

Der Kuchen, der lecker aussieht, ist selbstgemacht.
The cake that looks delicious is homemade.

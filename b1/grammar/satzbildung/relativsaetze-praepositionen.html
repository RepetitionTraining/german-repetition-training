Tips

  • Relative clauses can include prepositions

  • The preposition stands before the relative pronoun

  • The preposition determines the case of the relative pronoun

  • Common prepositions: mit, von, zu, bei, für, über, auf

  • The relative pronoun must match the gender and number of the noun

  • The verb in the relative clause goes to the end

Examples#

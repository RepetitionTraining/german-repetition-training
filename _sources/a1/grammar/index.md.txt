A systematic reference for A1-level German grammar. Each article covers one grammatical topic completely: rules, forms, examples, and common mistakes.

Use this section when you encounter a form you don't recognise and need a clear explanation. The topics below run from the noun system (articles, cases, pronouns) through the verb system to sentence structure, but you don't have to read them in order.

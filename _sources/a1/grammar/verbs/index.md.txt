# Verbs: Forms and Usage

Verbs change their ending for each person: *ich lerne*, *du lernst*, *er lernt*. This section covers the essential verbs *sein* and *haben*, regular and irregular present-tense forms, the modal verbs, separable verbs, verbs that demand a specific case, the imperative, and *es gibt*.

# Temporal Prepositions

Seven little words pin events to the clock and the calendar. Three of them you already use as chunks (*um, am, im*); this page adds *von ... bis*, *seit*, *nach*, and *vor*, and turns the chunks into a system.

## The big three: *um*, *am*, *im*

Each is locked to one kind of time word. There is no choosing, which makes them easy:

| Preposition | Goes with | Example |
|---|---|---|
| um | clock times | um neun Uhr |
| am | days, dates, parts of the day | am Montag, am ersten Mai, am Abend |
| im | months, seasons | im Januar, im Sommer |

**Examples:**

:::{div} ol-examples
Der Kurs beginnt {prep}`um` neun Uhr.
: The course starts at nine.

{prep}`Am` Wochenende habe ich frei.
: At the weekend I am off.

{prep}`Im` Sommer ist es warm.
: In summer it is warm.
:::

## Spans and durations: *von ... bis*, *seit*

*Von ... bis* frames a span; *seit* marks a starting point that still holds, always with the dative, always with the present tense.

**Examples:**

:::{div} ol-examples
Ich arbeite {prep}`von` neun {prep}`bis` fünf.
: I work from nine to five.

Ich lerne {prep}`seit` einem Jahr Deutsch.
: I have been learning German for a year.

{prep}`Seit` Montag bin ich krank.
: I have been ill since Monday.
:::

## Before and after: *vor*, *nach*

*Vor* (before) and *nach* (after) order events, both with the dative: *vor der Arbeit*, *nach dem Essen*.

**Examples:**

:::{div} ol-examples
{prep}`Nach` der Arbeit bin ich müde.
: After work I am tired.

{prep}`Vor` dem Frühstück dusche ich.
: Before breakfast I shower.
:::

## Summary

:::{admonition} Key points
:class: note

- *um* + clock, *am* + day/date, *im* + month/season: fixed pairs.
- *von ... bis* spans; *seit* + dative + present tense for ongoing states.
- *vor* and *nach* + dative order events.
- German uses present tense with *seit* where English uses "have been".
:::

:::{admonition} Common mistakes
:class: caution

- *\*Ich lernte seit einem Jahr.* → Ich **lerne** seit einem Jahr. (seit takes the present)
- *\*in Montag / an Januar* → **am** Montag, **im** Januar. (fixed pairs)
- *\*nach die Arbeit* → nach **der** Arbeit. (nach takes the dative)
:::

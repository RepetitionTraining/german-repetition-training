# Talking about family

After this page you can describe your family: who belongs to it, their names and ages, and a fact or two about each person. Family is one of the first topics Germans ask newcomers about, and one of the easiest to prepare.

## When to use it

- Answering *Hast du Geschwister?* or *Sind Sie verheiratet?*
- Talking about photos or telling someone about home
- Small talk with colleagues and neighbours

## Key phrases

| German | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ich habe einen Bruder. | I have a brother. | haben + accusative |
| Hast du Geschwister? | Do you have siblings? | |
| Ich bin Einzelkind. | I am an only child. | no article |
| Mein Vater / meine Mutter | my father / my mother | possessive articles |
| Ich bin verheiratet. | I am married. | |
| die Eltern, die Großeltern | parents, grandparents | plural only |

## Who is in the family

The core vocabulary: *der Vater, die Mutter, die Eltern; der Bruder, die Schwester, die Geschwister; der Sohn, die Tochter, die Kinder; der Opa, die Oma, die Großeltern; der Mann, die Frau* (also "husband" and "wife").

Saying who you have uses *haben*, and the accusative shows up naturally: *Ich habe **einen** Bruder.*

**Examples:**

:::{div} ol-examples
Ich habe {akk}`einen Bruder` und {akk}`eine Schwester`.
: I have a brother and a sister.

Ich habe zwei Kinder.
: I have two children.

Ich habe {akk}`keine Geschwister`.
: I have no siblings.
:::

## Describing your people

With the possessive articles you can pick out each person and say something about them: name, age, city, job, or what they like doing. Every sentence pattern you already know works here.

**Examples:**

:::{div} ol-examples
Mein Bruder heißt Jonas.
: My brother is called Jonas.

Meine Tochter ist fünf Jahre alt.
: My daughter is five years old.

Meine Frau kommt aus Brasilien.
: My wife comes from Brazil.
:::

:::{note}
*Mein Mann* and *meine Frau* mean "my husband" and "my wife". For unmarried partners, *mein Freund / meine Freundin* does the job, context making clear it is not just any friend.
:::

## Summary

:::{admonition} Key points
:class: note

- *Ich habe einen Bruder / eine Schwester / zwei Kinder* lists your family.
- Possessive articles introduce each person: *mein Vater, meine Mutter*.
- *Hast du Geschwister?* and *Sind Sie verheiratet?* are the standard questions.
- *Einzelkind*, like professions, takes no article.
:::

:::{admonition} Common mistakes
:class: caution

- *\*Ich habe ein Bruder.* → Ich habe **einen** Bruder. (haben takes the accusative)
- *\*Meine Vater* → **Mein** Vater. (Vater is masculine)
- *\*Ich bin ein Einzelkind.* → Ich bin **Einzelkind**. (no article, like professions)
:::

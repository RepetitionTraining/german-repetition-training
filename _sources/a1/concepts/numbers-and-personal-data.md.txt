# Numbers and personal data

After this page you can count, understand numbers when you hear them, and hand over your own details: phone number, age, street address, and postcode. Numbers show up in nearly every A1 situation, so this page pays for itself quickly.

## When to use it

- Giving your phone number or address at a desk or on a form
- Saying and understanding ages, house numbers, and postcodes
- Following prices and times later on (both build on these numbers)

## Key phrases

| German | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Wie ist Ihre Telefonnummer? | What is your phone number? | formal |
| Meine Nummer ist ... | My number is ... | digits read one by one |
| Wie alt sind Sie? / bist du? | How old are you? | formal / informal |
| Ich bin ... Jahre alt. | I am ... years old. | with a number |
| Meine Adresse ist ... | My address is ... | street + house number |
| die Postleitzahl | postcode | often read digit by digit |

## The numbers 0 to 20

The numbers up to twelve are their own words; from thirteen, the pattern is unit + *zehn*.

| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 null | 6 sechs | 11 elf | 16 sechzehn |
| 1 eins | 7 sieben | 12 zwölf | 17 siebzehn |
| 2 zwei | 8 acht | 13 dreizehn | 18 achtzehn |
| 3 drei | 9 neun | 14 vierzehn | 19 neunzehn |
| 4 vier | 10 zehn | 15 fünfzehn | 20 zwanzig |
| 5 fünf | | | |

Note the two irregular teens: *sechzehn* (not sechszehn) and *siebzehn* (not siebenzehn).

## Units before tens: *einundzwanzig*

From 21 upwards, German says the unit first, then the ten, joined by *und*: 21 is *einundzwanzig*, literally "one and twenty". This feels backwards at first; the track below drills it until it does not.

| | |
|---|---|
| 20 zwanzig | 60 sechzig |
| 30 dreißig | 70 siebzig |
| 40 vierzig | 80 achtzig |
| 50 fünfzig | 90 neunzig |

**Examples:**

:::{div} ol-examples
einundzwanzig
: twenty-one (one-and-twenty)

fünfundvierzig
: forty-five (five-and-forty)

neunundneunzig
: ninety-nine
:::

## Giving your details

Phone numbers are read digit by digit. Addresses put the house number after the street name, and the postcode before the city.

**Examples:**

:::{div} ol-examples
Meine Nummer ist null, eins, sieben, zwei.
: My number is zero, one, seven, two.

Ich wohne in der Bahnhofstraße zwölf.
: I live at Bahnhofstraße 12.

Ich bin einunddreißig Jahre alt.
: I am thirty-one years old.
:::

## Summary

:::{admonition} Key points
:class: note

- Learn 0-12 as words, 13-19 as unit + *zehn*, tens up to *hundert*.
- From 21: unit first, then ten: *einundzwanzig*.
- Phone numbers and postcodes are read digit by digit.
- Age uses *sein*: *Ich bin 30 Jahre alt.*
:::

:::{admonition} Common mistakes
:class: caution

- *\*zwanzigeins* → **einundzwanzig**. (unit before ten, joined with und)
- *\*sechszehn / siebenzehn* → **sechzehn / siebzehn**. (the s and en drop)
- *\*Ich habe 30 Jahre.* → Ich **bin** 30 Jahre alt. (age uses sein)
:::

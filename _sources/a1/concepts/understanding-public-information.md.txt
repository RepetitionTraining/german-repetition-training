# Understanding public information

After this page the signs, notices, and loudspeaker announcements around you start making sense: shop hours, station platforms, and the little imperatives on every door. This is a listening and reading skill; you rarely need to produce these phrases, only to catch them.

## When to use it

- Reading signs on shops, doors, and machines
- Catching announcements at stations and on trains
- Understanding notices about hours, closures, and prohibitions

## Key phrases

| German | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| geöffnet / geschlossen | open / closed | shop doors |
| Eingang / Ausgang | entrance / exit | |
| drücken / ziehen | push / pull | on doors |
| ... verboten | ... prohibited | Rauchen verboten |
| Achtung! / Vorsicht! | Attention! / Careful! | |
| das Gleis | the platform | stations |
| die Verspätung | the delay | |

## Doors, shops, and signs

Signs compress language to its bones: no articles, no full sentences. Opening hours run *von ... bis ...*, prohibitions end in *verboten*.

**Examples:**

:::{div} ol-examples
Geöffnet von neun bis achtzehn Uhr.
: Open from nine to six.

Heute geschlossen.
: Closed today.

Rauchen verboten.
: Smoking prohibited.
:::

## At the station

Announcements recycle the same handful of patterns with different numbers. Listen for the platform (*Gleis*), the delay (*Verspätung*), and the stop (*Halt*).

**Examples:**

:::{div} ol-examples
Der Zug nach München fährt von Gleis fünf.
: The train to Munich leaves from platform five.

Der Zug hat zehn Minuten Verspätung.
: The train is ten minutes late.

Nächster Halt: Hauptbahnhof.
: Next stop: main station.
:::

## Short official requests

*Bitte* plus an infinitive is the polite official voice: *Bitte hier warten. Bitte drücken.* You will see it on machines, counters, and doors everywhere.

**Examples:**

:::{div} ol-examples
Bitte hier warten.
: Please wait here.

Der Aufzug ist kaputt.
: The lift is out of order.
:::

## Summary

:::{admonition} Key points
:class: note

- Signs drop articles and verbs: *geöffnet, geschlossen, Eingang, Ausgang*.
- *von ... bis ...* frames opening hours; *verboten* ends prohibitions.
- Station survival: *Gleis*, *Verspätung*, *Nächster Halt*.
- *Bitte* + infinitive is the official request form: *Bitte drücken.*
:::

:::{admonition} Common mistakes
:class: caution

- Confusing *drücken* (push) and *ziehen* (pull): the daily door lottery; learn the pair.
- Hearing *Gleis fünf* but running to platform four: numbers under pressure need the drill from the numbers track.
- *Kein Trinkwasser* means the water is NOT drinkable; *kein* on a sign always warns.
:::

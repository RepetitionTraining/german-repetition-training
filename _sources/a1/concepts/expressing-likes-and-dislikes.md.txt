# Expressing likes and dislikes

After this page you can talk about what you enjoy: activities with *gern*, things with *mögen*, and the negative of both. This unlocks hobby talk, food preferences, and most small talk beyond introductions.

## When to use it

- Answering *Was machst du gern?* about hobbies and free time
- Saying what food and drink you like or avoid
- Reacting to suggestions with a clear like or dislike

## Key phrases

| German | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ich koche gern. | I like cooking. | gern after the verb |
| Ich arbeite nicht gern. | I don't like working. | nicht gern |
| Ich mag Kaffee. | I like coffee. | mögen + thing |
| Ich mag keinen Fisch. | I don't like fish. | mögen + kein |
| Magst du ...? | Do you like ...? | informal |
| Mögen Sie ...? | Do you like ...? | formal |
| Das gefällt mir. | I like that. | fixed chunk |

## Liking activities: *gern*

German has no verb for "to like doing". Instead, take the activity verb and drop *gern* after it: *Ich koche gern*, literally "I cook gladly". The negative puts *nicht* before *gern*.

**Examples:**

:::{div} ol-examples
Ich spiele **gern** Fußball.
: I like playing football.

Sie tanzt **gern**.
: She likes dancing.

Ich arbeite **nicht gern** am Wochenende.
: I don't like working at the weekend.
:::

## Liking things: *mögen*

For nouns, use *mögen*: *Ich mag Kaffee*. The forms you need now: *ich mag, du magst, er/sie mag, Sie mögen*. The negative uses *kein*: *Ich mag keinen Fisch*.

**Examples:**

:::{div} ol-examples
Ich mag {akk}`den Sommer`.
: I like summer.

Er mag Tiere.
: He likes animals.

Ich mag {akk}`keinen Fisch`.
: I don't like fish.
:::

:::{tip}
*Gern* for doing, *mögen* for things. If there is an activity verb in the sentence, reach for *gern*; if only a noun, reach for *mögen*.
:::

## Summary

:::{admonition} Key points
:class: note

- Activities: verb + *gern*. *Ich lese gern.*
- Things: *mögen*. *Ich mag Kaffee.*
- Negatives: *nicht gern* for activities, *mögen + kein* for things.
- *Was machst du gern?* is the hobby question to expect and to ask.
:::

:::{admonition} Common mistakes
:class: caution

- *\*Ich gern koche.* → Ich koche **gern**. (gern comes after the verb)
- *\*Ich mag schwimmen gern.* → Ich schwimme **gern**. (activity: gern, not mögen)
- *\*Ich mag nicht Fisch.* → Ich mag **keinen** Fisch. (nouns are negated with kein)
:::

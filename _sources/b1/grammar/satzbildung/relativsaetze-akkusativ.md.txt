:::{admonition} Tips
:class: tip

- Accusative relative pronouns: den, die, das, die (plural)
- Used when the relative pronoun is the direct object of the relative clause
- Masculine changes from der to den in accusative
- Feminine, neuter, and plural remain the same as nominative
- The verb in the relative clause goes to the end
- Common with verbs that take accusative objects

:::

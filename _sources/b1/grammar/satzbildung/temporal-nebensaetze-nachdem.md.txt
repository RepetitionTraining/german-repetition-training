:::{admonition} Tips
:class: tip

- Nachdem introduces a temporal clause meaning "after"
- The verb in the nachdem clause goes to the end
- Important: Uses a different tense than the main clause
- Main clause in present → nachdem clause in perfect
- Main clause in past → nachdem clause in pluperfect
- Describes what happened before the main action

:::

Präpositionen mit Genitiv#

Using genitive prepositions for time, place, reason, and contrast at B2

See also

  • B2 > Grammar > Konnektor während

  • B2 > Grammar > Nominalisierung

  • B1 > Grammar > Genitiv

  • B1 > Grammar > wegen und trotz

  • B1 > Grammar > Zeitangaben: Präpositionen

At B2, genitive prepositions help you sound more precise and more formal, especially in reports, academic writing, and professional communication. You can use them to locate events in time, describe boundaries in space, explain causes, and express contrast. In everyday speech, several of these prepositions are often used with dative, but written standard German usually prefers genitive.

Examples:

  • Within two weeks, we will have a decision.

  • Despite the criticism, the team continued the project.

  • Due to technical problems, the event started late.

Form patterns and register#

Most prepositions in this lesson are followed by a genitive noun phrase in standard written German. This means the article and often the noun form show genitive marking.

Gender/number

Typical article in genitive

Example phrase

Masculine

des + noun + -(e)s

wegen des Zugausfalls

Neuter

des + noun + -(e)s

während des Meetings

Feminine

der

trotz der Kritik

Plural

der

innerhalb der Fristen

Examples:

  • Wegen des Streiks kam es zu Verspätungen.
    Because of the strike, there were delays.

  • Trotz der Unsicherheit investiert die Firma weiter.
    Despite the uncertainty, the company continues to invest.

  • Innerhalb der nächsten Woche klären wir die offenen Punkte.
    Within the next week, we will clarify the open points.

  • Während des Gesprächs wurden neue Prioritäten festgelegt.
    During the discussion, new priorities were set.

Note

In spoken German, wegen, trotz, während, and dank are often used with dative, for example: wegen dem schlechten Wetter. At B2, keep genitive as your default in formal writing.

Zeit und Ort: während, innerhalb, außerhalb#

These prepositions let you define temporal windows and spatial limits very clearly. Während focuses on simultaneity in a time span, while innerhalb and außerhalb set boundaries in time or space.

Preposition

Core meaning

Typical use

während

during

time period in progress

innerhalb

within

inside a time limit or area

außerhalb

outside of

outside a time frame or area

Examples:

  • Während des Umzugs arbeitete das Team im Homeoffice.
    During the move, the team worked from home.

  • Innerhalb der Probezeit erhielt sie bereits mehr Verantwortung.
    Within the probation period, she already received more responsibility.

  • Außerhalb der Geschäftszeiten antwortet nur der Notdienst.
    Outside business hours, only the emergency service responds.

  • Innerhalb des Stadtzentrums gilt Tempo 30.
    Within the city center, a 30 km/h speed limit applies.

  • Außerhalb der EU gelten andere Zollregeln.
    Outside the EU, different customs rules apply.

Warning

Do not confuse prepositional während + noun phrase with conjunction während + clause.

  • Preposition: Während des Vortrags blieb es ruhig.

  • Conjunction: Während der Vortrag lief, machten sich viele Notizen.

Grund und Folge: wegen, dank, infolge, aufgrund#

These prepositions are useful when you want compact, noun-based cause statements. At B2, this style appears frequently in written communication because it sounds concise and structured.

Preposition

Meaning

Register tendency

wegen

because of

neutral, frequent in speech and writing

dank

thanks to

often positive, but can be neutral/ironic in context

infolge

as a result of

formal, written

aufgrund

due to / on the basis of

formal, written

Examples:

  • Wegen des Serverproblems wurde die Anmeldung unterbrochen.
    Because of the server problem, registration was interrupted.

  • Dank der klaren Struktur war der Bericht leicht nachvollziehbar.
    Thanks to the clear structure, the report was easy to follow.

  • Infolge des Starkregens mussten mehrere Straßen gesperrt werden.
    As a result of heavy rain, several roads had to be closed.

  • Aufgrund der neuen Daten hat die Behörde ihre Empfehlung angepasst.
    Due to the new data, the authority adjusted its recommendation.

  • Wegen der vielen Rückfragen verschob sich die Entscheidung um eine Woche.
    Because of many follow-up questions, the decision was delayed by a week.

Tip

If you need a more conversational tone, replace a prepositional phrase with a clause: wegen des Staus -> weil es einen Stau gab. For formal summaries, the noun phrase is often preferable.

Gegengrund: trotz#

Trotz introduces a contrast: something happens even though an obstacle exists. This is especially useful in arguments, reports, and discussion writing.

Trotz + genitive noun phrase, main statement

Examples:

  • Trotz des Widerstands setzte die Stadt den Plan um.
    Despite the resistance, the city implemented the plan.

  • Trotz der hohen Kosten blieb das Angebot attraktiv.
    Despite the high costs, the offer remained attractive.

  • Trotz des Zeitdrucks arbeitete das Team sehr sorgfältig.
    Despite the time pressure, the team worked very carefully.

  • Trotz der kritischen Rückmeldungen veröffentlichte sie den Artikel.
    Despite critical feedback, she published the article.

Note

In everyday speech, many people say trotz dem Problem. For formal writing and exams, use the genitive form: trotz des Problems.

Summary#

Key points

  • Genitive prepositions at this level let you express time, place, cause, and contrast in a compact way.

  • während, innerhalb, and außerhalb are central for temporal and spatial boundaries.

  • wegen, dank, infolge, and aufgrund build noun-based cause statements.

  • trotz introduces a clear counter-reason.

  • Spoken German often uses dative after wegen, dank, während, and trotz, but formal writing usually prefers genitive.

Common mistakes

  • wegen dem starken Regen -> wegen des starken Regens. (Use genitive in standard written German.)

  • während wir der Sitzung -> während der Sitzung. (Preposition needs a noun phrase; conjunction needs a clause.)

  • innerhalb den Fristen -> innerhalb der Fristen. (Plural genitive article is der.)

  • aufgrund des neue Daten -> aufgrund der neuen Daten. (Here the noun is plural: use der + adjective -en.)

  • trotz des Probleme -> trotz des Problems. (Masculine/neuter singular usually needs noun ending -(e)s.)
